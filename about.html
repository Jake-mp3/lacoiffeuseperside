About

Located in the heart of Wakefield, Quebec, this hair salon has been offering a warm and welcoming space for 15 years, where every client feels at ease. With attentive listening and a deep understanding of each person's needs, Perside the hairstylist—specializing in color and cutting—creates a personalized, intimate experience. Whether you're looking to transform your look or simply refresh your style, you'll find professional, caring service here. Open to all, the salon welcomes a diverse clientele, providing each person with a relaxing and tailored care experience.
